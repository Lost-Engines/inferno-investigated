# Credits and scope

Inferno was created by Digital Image Design and published by Ocean in 1994. The pilot’s handbook credits Shaun Hollywood with concept, design and direction, and Nik and Mrs Fiend of Alien Sex Fiend with the soundtrack. Original game models, maps, pictures and music belong to their respective creators.

The article, presentation and adapted research viewers are work by the Lost Engines Project. This is an independent investigation, without affiliation or endorsement.

The site includes selected decoded display data, illustrations and short excerpts of synthesizer renders for interactive study. It offers no game archive, executable, install media, ROM, original-resource downloads or model exports. It does not grant permission to redistribute the original game content.

Music excerpts are software-emulator renders, not recordings of physical cards. No synthesizer firmware is included. The site’s layout follows TFX Traced and Darker Discovered. The marked and parse5 dependencies retain their MIT licences.
